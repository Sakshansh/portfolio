About Me

I'm Sakshanshu Kumar, a Computer Science undergraduate pursuing my B.Tech. Originally from Aligarh, I currently live in Ghaziabad, where I spend most of my time learning how software is built — and then building it myself.

My foundation is in C++ and Python, and I enjoy problem solving and data structures. I've also explored web development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, built a small iOS app in Swift, and I'm currently learning cloud computing with AWS.

I believe in learning by doing: understand the concept, dry-run it, then write the code. I'm looking for opportunities to grow as a developer and contribute to meaningful projects.
